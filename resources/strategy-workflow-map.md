# Strategy Workflow Map

A backcasting template that helps leaders and teams map their strategy from a long-term destination back to this year.

## North star

- What future destination are you working toward?
- What will be different for your organization, community, or clients when you arrive?

## Year-by-year path

- What must be true one year before that destination?
- What must be true two years before that destination?
- What must be true this year to stay oriented toward the future you named?

## Priorities

- What work matters most right now?
- What can wait?
- What should be stopped because it no longer serves the destination?

## Adjustment points

- What signals will tell you the plan needs to shift?
- How will the team adapt without losing the way?
