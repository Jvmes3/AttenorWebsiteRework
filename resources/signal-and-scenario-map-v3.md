# Signal and Scenario Map: A Leader's Guide

A visual tool for leaders who want to know whether their current strategy is built for the world they are actually heading into.

## 1. Read emerging signals

- What is changing in your industry, community, or operating environment?
- Which shifts are still faint but gaining momentum?
- What are your clients, partners, or competitors beginning to notice?

## 2. Test assumptions

- What is your current strategy assuming will stay stable?
- Which assumptions would be costly if they turned out to be wrong?
- Where are you relying on outdated information?

## 3. Map possible scenarios

- What future are you actively preparing for?
- What future would disrupt your current plans?
- What future would create a new opening for leadership?

## 4. Name strategic tension

- Where is the gap between your current path and the world you may be heading into?
- What decision needs clearer future context?

## 5. Identify the next move

- What evidence, conversation, or experiment would reduce uncertainty?
- What move would help you prepare without overcommitting too early?
